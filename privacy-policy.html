PaceUp RPG — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

PaceUp RPG is a camera-based fitness game: it counts your push-ups, squats, and other exercises by watching your movement through the phone's camera. This policy explains exactly what the app does with that camera feed and with your data. In short: your camera images never leave your device unless you choose to share them.

The camera is processed entirely on your phone. Frames are analysed in real time to estimate your body pose and count reps, then discarded. The app never uploads camera images. The only video it keeps is a set clip, which is off unless you turn it on (see below), and it stays on your phone unless you share it yourself.

What the app accesses

  • Camera — to detect and count your reps. Live frames are analysed on-device and immediately discarded. Nothing from the camera is transmitted.
  • Set clips (optional, off by default) — if you turn on Record clips in Settings, the app records the screen during a set (using your phone's screen-recording feature, which asks for your permission) so you can watch or share the set afterwards. The clip is saved on your phone only. It is never uploaded by the app; it leaves your phone only if you share it yourself, for example with the share button. A new set replaces the previous clip.
  • Photos (optional) — you can add a photo from your library to a training-journal entry. The app sees only the photo you pick, copies it into the journal on your phone, and never uploads it.
  • Notifications (optional) — to send a daily reminder to train. You can deny or revoke this at any time in system settings.
  • Accessibility service — Rep Lock (optional, Android only) — if you turn on Rep Lock and enable its accessibility service, PaceUp RPG reads only the package name of the app in the foreground (for example com.instagram.android) each time you switch apps, to decide whether to show the lock screen for an app you chose to lock. It cannot read anything on your screen. The package name is checked on your device and discarded: it is never stored, never sent to our servers or anyone else, and never used for advertising or analytics. The list of apps you chose and your unlock times stay on your device. Turn the service off in Android's Accessibility settings at any time.

What is stored on your device

Your progress — level, rep totals, streak, quests, medals, rivals, settings, and cosmetic purchases — is saved locally on your device only. So are your training journal (notes, photos you added, and set clips you kept) and the latest set clip. Uninstalling the app removes all of it; you can also delete journal entries in the app.

What leaves your device (only if you opt in)

PaceUp RPG can optionally sync progress and post to leaderboards. If — and only if — you enable cloud sync, the app sends aggregate numbers (such as total reps, best depth, duel wins) and the display name you choose, to our backend so leaderboards work across devices. Camera imagery is never part of this and is never uploaded. If you never enable cloud sync, no data leaves your device at all.

Community features (optional)

These only work with cloud sync, and each one is something you choose to use.

  • Guild chat. Messages you post in a guild are stored on our backend with the display name you chose and a timestamp, and are visible only to members of that guild. You can delete your own messages (the text is erased). You can report a message: the report, a copy of the reported text and the reason you picked are kept for our moderators, who review reports within 24 hours and remove content or suspend accounts that break the rules. A message reported by three different members is hidden straight away, before a moderator has looked at it; if it turns out to be fine it is restored. Blocking someone hides their messages on your device only — nobody, including us, is told who you blocked.
  • Community events and badges. If you join an event, the camera-counted reps you publish while it runs are added to your event progress, and any badge you earn is stored with your account.
  • Optional profile fields for leaderboard filters. In Settings › Account you can add your country (a two-letter code, pre-filled from your phone's region but not sent until you tap Save), an age band (18–24, 25–34, 35–44, 45–54, 55+) and your sex (female or male). Each one is optional and has a “prefer not to say” choice. Your country is public: it is shown next to your name on leaderboards. Your age band and sex are never shown to anyone; they are used only to filter leaderboards, and only you can filter by your own values. We never ask for your exact age or date of birth. Clear the fields and save to remove them from our backend.

Apple Health and Health Connect (optional)

PaceUp RPG can save each finished set to your phone's health store — Apple Health on iOS, Health Connect on Android. This is off until you turn it on in Settings, and you can turn it off again at any time.

PaceUp RPG only writes. It never reads.

The app requests permission to write workouts (iOS) / exercise sessions (Android) and requests no read permission of any kind. PaceUp RPG cannot see your steps, heart rate, weight, sleep, or any workout recorded by another app — including workouts PaceUp RPG itself wrote earlier.

When enabled, each finished set writes exactly three things:

  • which movement you did (for example, push-ups);
  • how long the set lasted, measured from your first counted rep to your last;
  • how many reps were counted.

And specifically:

  • Health data is never sent to PaceUp RPG's servers. It travels one way: from the app into your phone's health store. It is not part of cloud sync, is not written to iCloud, and is not included in any backup PaceUp RPG makes.
  • Health data is never used for advertising, and is never shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party.
  • No part of PaceUp RPG is locked behind it. No feature, no subscription, and no part of the game depends on granting health access. Declining changes nothing except that sets stop being written.
  • PaceUp RPG does not estimate calories burned. It has no way to know your energy expenditure from a camera, so it does not write a guess into your health record.

Sets that were already written belong to you, and PaceUp RPG does not delete them. Turning the setting off stops any further writing; to remove past entries, use Apple Health or Health Connect directly, where you can also revoke PaceUp RPG's permission entirely at any time.

In-app purchases

Cosmetic skins are sold through Google Play. Payments are handled by Google; PaceUp RPG never sees or stores your payment details. Purchases are cosmetic only and do not affect gameplay.

Children

PaceUp RPG is a general-audience fitness app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It contains no ads. Guild chat is limited to short text messages between members of the same guild, with reporting and blocking built in.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes all local data. If you enabled cloud sync and want your synced records removed, use Delete account in Settings, or contact us (see Contact below) and we will delete them. Deleting your account also deletes your guild messages, event progress, badges and the optional profile fields.
